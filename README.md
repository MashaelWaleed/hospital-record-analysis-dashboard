# 🏥 Hospital Record Analysis Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive healthcare data analysis dashboard built in **Microsoft Excel** to track patient outcomes, risk stratification, and monthly mortality trends across 403 clinical records.
---

## 🛠️ Dashboard Features & Visuals
* **KPI Summary Cards:** High-level tracking of total patient volume, survival count/rate, and mortality count/rate.
* **Interactive Controls:** Dynamic slicers for **Outcome** (*Die / Live*), **Risk Category** (*High / Low*), and an interactive **Check-in Date Timeline** for period filtering.
* **Analytical Charts:**
  * *100% Stacked Column Chart:* Monthly patient outcome proportions (Live vs. Die).
  * *Donut & Pie Charts:* Risk category split and overall patient outcome distribution.
  * *Combo Chart (Column + Line):* Monthly mortality count alongside cumulative mortality growth (reaching 69 by December).
  * *Column & Waterfall Charts:* Month-over-month percentage change and net difference in mortality[cite: 1].
